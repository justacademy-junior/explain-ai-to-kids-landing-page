So, what is artificial intelligence?

Artificial intelligence is about teaching computers to do jobs that usually need human thinking, like understanding speech, spotting faces or suggesting a song. The field of artificial intelligence is big, but the idea for a child is small.

🎓

It practises

AI learns by looking at lots and lots of examples, the way you learned to spot a dog after meeting many dogs.

🧩

It finds patterns

Machine learning and artificial intelligence work together. The computer studies examples and finds the pattern by itself.

🙅

It is not alive

A voice assistant can sound friendly, but it has no feelings, no dreams and no friends. It is a very clever program.

Say it out loud at home"AI is a computer that practised a lot, and now it is good at one clever job."

How to explain AI at every age

A five year old and a thirteen year old need different words. Pick your child's age and try these.

💬 Say this

"A computer can learn like a puppy learns tricks. It tries, gets a little better, and tries again."

🧸 Try this

Sort a basket of toys by colour. Then explain that computers sort photos the same way, just with thousands of pictures.

⏱️ Keep it short

Two sentences are plenty. If your child wants more, they will ask. Children always do.

💬 Say this

"A video app notices what you watched and finds similar videos. It learned that pattern from lots of people."

🔍 Try this

Ask "How does it know?" together. Explain that AI learns what a dog looks like from thousands of dog pictures.

⚖️ Talk about mistakes

If AI only saw small brown dogs, it might miss a big white one. Kids learn fairness fast.

💬 Say this

"AI learns from data, and data can be incomplete or unfair. People have to check it."

📱 Try this

Look at how their social feed is chosen. Ask why some videos keep coming back.

🧠 Ask this

Can a machine be truly creative, or does it only remix? Who is responsible when AI gets it wrong?

A five day plan, ten minutes a day

No special equipment. Just curiosity, a notebook and a snack.

🔎

Monday

Spot the AI at home. Make a list together.

🍎

Tuesday

Teach the computer with a paper fruit sorting game.

🌦️

Wednesday

Collect data. Track the weather and guess tomorrow.

💬

Thursday

Ask a chatbot a question, then check the answer in a book.

🎨

Friday

Draw a dragon, then compare it with an AI picture.

Keep it safe, honest and fun

Think of this as the digital version of looking both ways before crossing the street.

  • 🔒Never share a full name, address, school or password with a chatbot.
  • ✅AI can be wrong. Make "let's check that" a family habit.
  • ✏️Try first, then use AI to check. If a tool does the homework, nothing is learned.
  • 🌳Balance screens with drawing, reading and outdoor play.
